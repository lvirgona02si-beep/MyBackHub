Frequently Asked Questions Answered…

What will I walk away with?
The three secrets in full, demonstrated rather than described, plus a clear picture of why the therapies you’ve already tried didn’t hold. You’ll leave knowing what your next step is, whether or not you ever work with us.
I’ve tried PT, chiropractic care and apps. Why is this different?
Because those are built around a straight spine. Scoliosis curves and rotates, and symmetrical exercise loads both sides of an asymmetrical spine equally, which is why the relief fades. The masterclass covers what to do instead.
I’m older, or my curve is severe. Is this relevant to me?
Yes. The method is built around the curve you have now, not the one you had at diagnosis. We cover severe curves, post-fusion spines and patients well past 60 directly in the session.

Scoliosis specifically. General back-pain advice is most of the problem we’re addressing. It’s the reason so many people with a curve spend years doing exercises that were never designed for them.

No. Common stretches are symmetrical, and that’s precisely what doesn’t work here. What we walk through is curve-specific and positional.
